Biography

The band

Crossfield Bend is a three-piece Americana band with fiddle, guitars, and harmonica at its core, playing roots music that sits where the Rolling Stones, John Prine, Guy Clark, Springsteen and Townes Van Zandt meet, with country and celtic threads running through.

The songs are built to be played in a room full of people: plainspoken, warm, and made to move a crowd. The band holds a regular Tuesday Night Music Club residency at Roc'n Doc's in Port Credit and plays bars, legions, festivals and private events across the Greater Toronto Area.

Between the three of them, the members have opened for and shared stages with some of the biggest names in the business, and bring festival-stage experience to every room they play. No filler, no wasted motion. Just songs that carry a night.

The players

Three players, a handful of strings

Allison Leroux

Fiddle · Vocals

Has opened for The Chicks, Gowan and Dallas Smith, and played Boots & Hearts, North of Nowhere, the Toronto Folk Festival, the Southside Shuffle and the Tweed Stampede. Shared stages with Sophie B. Hawkins and Paula Cole, and plays regularly with Downchild's Chuck Jackson, Suzie McNeil and Tom Barlow. Horseshoe Tavern alum. 2024 Lieutenant Governor's Ontario Heritage Award for Youth Achievement; Mississauga Arts Awards (MARTY) nominee. Member, Canadian Federation of Musicians (AFM). Old-time, French-Canadian, celtic and bluegrass fiddle, and a square-dance caller.

Maurie Swan

Guitar · Vocals

Guitar and lead vocals. A fixture on the Southside Shuffle and the Port Credit circuit. Full bio on request.

Kevin McConnell

Technical

Stage plot & input list

We keep it simple. We are fully self-contained: we bring our own PA, a Bose L1 Pro Compact with a Bose mixer (plus a smaller rig for tighter rooms), and we mix ourselves from the stage, with the PA set level with or just in front of us. No monitors; we run off our own stage sound, so load-in is light and sound check is quick. What we need from you is two power outlets near the stage and a playing area about 10 by 8 feet. Running your own front of house? We will hand your engineer a clean stereo line from our mixer, two channels, already mixed. Would you rather we just run the room? Happy to.
