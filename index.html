The Band

Three players, a handful of strings

Crossfield Bend is a three-piece Americana band with fiddle, guitars, and harmonica at its core. The songs sit where roots music lives: early Rolling Stones swagger, the plainspoken truth of John Prine and Guy Clark, the open-road pull of Springsteen, and the quiet ache of Townes Van Zandt, with country and celtic threads running through. No filler, no wasted motion. Just songs built to carry a room.

FAQ

Good to know
Do you bring your own sound?

Yes. We bring our own Bose L1 PA and mix ourselves from the stage. All we need is two power outlets.

How much room do you need?

About 10 by 8 feet. Patios, bars, halls and living rooms all work.

How long do you play?

It is flexible, from a single set to a full evening. Tell us what you have in mind.

Can you bring a bigger band?

Every so often we go out as a five-piece with bass and drums. Ask when you book.

Where do you play?

Mississauga, Port Credit, Brampton and across the Greater Toronto Area.

What does it cost?

It depends on the date, the length and the place. Pick a budget range in the booking form and we will reply with a quote.

What kind of music is it?

Americana and roots: early Rolling Stones, John Prine, Guy Clark, Springsteen and Townes Van Zandt, with country and celtic running through it.
