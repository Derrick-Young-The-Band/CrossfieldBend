The Band

Three players, a handful of strings

Crossfield Bend is a three-piece Americana band with fiddle, guitars, and harmonica at its core. The songs sit where roots music lives: early Rolling Stones swagger, the plainspoken truth of John Prine and Guy Clark, the open-road pull of Springsteen, and the quiet ache of Townes Van Zandt, with country and celtic threads running through. No filler, no wasted motion. Just songs built to carry a room.

2026 Mississauga Arts Awards · Emerging Artist nominee Southside Shuffle · festival stage
Where we have played Roc'n Doc's · Port Credit Legion · Donegal Arms · The Ivy Bridge · ANAF · Soranno's
