Technical Rider
Americana roots trio · crossfieldbend.com
We keep it simple. We are fully self-contained: we bring our own PA, a Bose L1 Pro Compact with a Bose mixer (plus a smaller rig for tighter rooms), and we mix ourselves from the stage, with the PA set level with or just in front of us. No monitors; we run off our own stage sound, so load-in is light and sound check is quick. What we need from you is two power outlets near the stage and a playing area about 10 by 8 feet. Running your own front of house? We will hand your engineer a clean stereo line from our mixer, two channels, already mixed, or we are glad to run the room ourselves.
